Writing I Am That I Am

Notes, fragments, structural experiments, character threads, and process reflections from an ongoing multi-POV novel about identity, reality, violence, memory, and convergence.

Status: In progress Form: Experimental novel Structure: Multiple POVs

About the Project

Ten lives. One structure. Something underneath all of them.

I Am That I Am is an experimental multi-POV novel built from separate lives that gradually begin to echo, distort, and converge.

The characters are literal, separate people with their own histories, desires, fears, and circumstances. Their stories do not begin as pieces of one obvious shared plot.

But patterns begin to repeat across them. Images recur. Language shifts. Events seem to rhyme with one another. The boundaries between individual experience and something larger become increasingly difficult to trust.

The project lives somewhere between literary fiction, psychological horror, existential dread, and structural experimentation — but the human stories come first.

The Structure

Ten points of view, each looking at a different edge of the same thing.

The Artist

Creation, perception, authorship, and the dangerous intimacy of making something visible.

The Child

Innocence, fear, memory, and the strange logic of a world that adults insist makes sense.

The Dealer

Desire, exchange, appetite, consequence, and the economies people build around need.

The Dog

Instinct, loyalty, perception, and a consciousness that does not organize reality the way humans do.

The Politician

Power, performance, identity, control, and the gap between public narrative and private reality.

The Deer

Vulnerability, animal awareness, movement, danger, and the unknowable experience of being watched.

The Traveler

Distance, displacement, movement, uncertainty, and the possibility that arrival may be its own kind of loss.

The Killer

Violence, intention, self-narration, consequence, and the stories people tell themselves about what they have done.

The Form

Shape, pattern, structure, emergence, and the unsettling possibility that form itself may have agency.

IS NOT

Absence, negation, collapse, and whatever remains when definition itself stops behaving.

Where It Is Now

Part manuscript, part map, part unfinished argument with reality.

A substantial portion of the novel exists in drafted form, alongside outlines, structural notes, scene ideas, and later material that is still being developed.

The current challenge is not finding the project. It already knows what it is. The challenge is returning to it, following the threads back into the manuscript, and continuing the convergence without flattening the distinct lives that make the structure work.

Returning

Giving the novel a place to exist again

One reason for building this project journal is practical: I want the novel visible enough that I keep thinking about it.

Creative projects have a strange way of disappearing when they stop having a physical or public presence. This page is partly an archive and partly a breadcrumb trail back into the manuscript.

Development

Structure

Keeping ten narrators distinct

The novel depends on each point of view feeling like a real consciousness rather than a symbolic placeholder.

The larger patterns matter, but they only work if the characters remain convincingly separate people whose lives can stand on their own before the echoes between them become impossible to ignore.

Early Development

Core idea

When separate stories begin to rhyme

The project began with the idea that people could be completely separate from one another and still participate in the same larger pattern.

Not because they secretly know each other. Not because everything needs to resolve into one tidy plot. The interest is in repetition, correspondence, and the uneasy feeling that different lives may be expressing variations of something larger than any one of them.
