About

A writer who refuses to pick just one container.

I’m interested in stories that linger — the strange family story, the essay that won’t stop changing shape, the character who needs a song instead of a paragraph.

My work moves between forms because the form is part of the storytelling. Sometimes that means prose. Sometimes it means music, illustration, conversation, live process, or something I haven’t figured out how to name yet. My work pushes against norms because rejection of the established expectation is what feels truest to me.

This site is a home for the finished work, the work-in-progress, and the messy, useful space in between.
